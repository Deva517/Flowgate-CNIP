CNIP concepts used

Packet switching

Represents a video stream as numbered packets.

Leaky Bucket

Finite queue; constant output rate; overflow packets are dropped.

Sliding Window

Limits how many unacknowledged packets can be in flight.

Go-Back-N ARQ

Receiver accepts in-order packets; after a gap, sender retries from the missing packet.

TCP vs UDP

Compares a recovery-based model with best-effort delivery.
